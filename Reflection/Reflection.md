# Reflection

Complete this section in the student's own words before submission.

## Suggested Structure

### Learning Progress

Explain how the work developed from basic Kotlin and Android Studio setup into Compose UI, list-based Material apps, navigation, and app architecture.

### Challenges

Describe the most difficult parts of the learning path. Good examples include Kotlin syntax, Gradle setup, state management, layout design, navigation, or testing.

### Solutions

Explain how those challenges were handled. Use specific examples from the student's own Android projects.

### Technical Justification

Discuss why certain Android development techniques were chosen, such as Compose, `LazyColumn`, Material Design, ViewModel, or Navigation Compose.

### Future Improvement

Identify what should be improved in future Android work, such as better testing, clearer architecture, accessibility, error handling, or support for different screen sizes.
